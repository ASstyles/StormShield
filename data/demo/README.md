# STORMSHIELD X — SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA REGISTRY

> **NOTICE: DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY**  
> All datasets in this directory are calibrated synthetic simulation models representing Coastal Andhra Pradesh geography.  
> They are strictly designed for hackathon demonstration, disaster digital twin simulation, and resilience testing.  
> **NEVER** present these simulated values as official real-time meteorological forecasts or government evacuation orders. Official advisories must be sourced directly from the **India Meteorological Department (IMD)** and the **National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)**.

## Contents
* `cyclone.json`: Synthetic parameters for Cyclone Jal-26 approaching Kakinada, AP.
* `hazard_zones.json`: Multi-hazard risk polygons across Kakinada, Visakhapatnam, Konaseema, and Krishna.
* `hospitals.json`: Critical healthcare centers with bed capacities and backup power statuses.
* `shelters.json`: Designated cyclone relief centers and evacuation shelters.
* `power_stations.json`: Primary transmission substations and electrical distribution nodes.
* `roads.json`: Evacuation route network links with flood vulnerability attributes.
* `population.json`: Aggregated demographic exposure blocks (1km² grids).
* `elevation.json`: 30m Digital Elevation Model (DEM) control points along the Bay of Bengal coastline.
